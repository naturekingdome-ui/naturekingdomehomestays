Chikmagalur is one of the easier hill trips from Bangalore. It is a half day drive on mostly good highway, with the climb into the coffee hills saved for the last stretch. Most people do it as a weekend, leaving Friday night or early Saturday and coming back Sunday evening.

Here is how to make the journey, with the numbers to check before you go.

# How Far and How Long

Bangalore to Chikmagalur is roughly 245 to 250 km. Plan for about 4.5 to 6 hours behind the wheel, more if you stop for a long breakfast or hit weekend traffic getting out of the city. The last 30 to 40 km, once you leave the highway and start climbing, are slower than the open road before it.

# The Two Main Routes

Route 1, via Kunigal and Hassan

Bangalore, Nelamangala, Kunigal, Channarayapatna, Hassan, Belur, then up to Chikmagalur. This is the route most drivers pick. It is largely four lane, the surface is good, and Belur near the end gives you a natural place to break the drive or add a temple stop.

Route 2, via Tiptur and Arsikere

Bangalore, Nelamangala, Yediyur, Tiptur, Arsikere, Kadur, Chikmagalur. Similar distance, sometimes lighter traffic, passing through coconut country. Road quality can vary in patches. Useful as an alternative if the Hassan route is congested.

Both routes start the same way, heading out of Bangalore towards Nelamangala, so the split comes later.

# Where to Stop

- Around Kunigal or Nelamangala for an early breakfast if you left the city before dawn. There are well known highway restaurants along this stretch serving idli, dosa and filter coffee.
- Channarayapatna and Hassan for fuel, restrooms and a proper meal.
- Belur, if you take Route 1 and have an hour, for the Chennakeshava temple. It is a short detour and breaks up the final climb.
- Once you start the ascent, viewpoints open up on the roadside. Pull over safely for the first proper look at the coffee hills.

# Leaving at the Right Time

- Friday night: Beats the Saturday morning exodus, but you arrive tired and in the dark on unfamiliar hill roads. Fine if you are a confident night driver.
- Saturday 5:00 to 6:00 AM: The sweet spot. You clear the city before traffic builds, stop for breakfast on the highway, and reach the estates with the day still ahead.
- Saturday mid morning: Workable, but you will share the road with everyone else and arrive mid afternoon.

For the return, leaving Chikmagalur by 3:00 to 4:00 PM on Sunday gets you back to Bangalore at a reasonable hour and ahead of the worst of the re entry traffic.

# By Train

The nearest railheads are at Kadur and Chikmagalur town. Services and timings change, so check a current schedule. From Kadur you will need a taxi or bus for the last stretch. Train is a relaxed option if the timings line up, but it gives you less flexibility once you are there, since most of the sights need a vehicle.

# By Bus

KSRTC and several private operators run overnight buses from Bangalore to Chikmagalur, including sleeper and semi sleeper coaches. You board late evening and arrive early morning. Confirm current schedules and book ahead on weekends and holidays. As with the train, plan how you will get around once you arrive, either local jeeps, hired taxis or a stay that can arrange trips.

# Driving Tips for the Hill Section

- Fuel up in Hassan or Kadur. Pumps thin out as you climb.
- The last stretch has tight bends and occasional livestock on the road. Keep your speed down.
- In the monsoon, expect fog patches and slow going. Drive with lights on.
- Download offline maps. Network drops in and out on the estates.

# Arriving

Most estate stays sit a short drive beyond Chikmagalur town, so factor in another 20 to 40 minutes from the town centre depending on where you are booked. Nature Kingdom is among the coffee plantations near Mallenahalli, with parking on site and space to arrive, stretch and start slowing down. Check in is from 12:00 PM, which suits an early Saturday departure from Bangalore, and check out is 11:00 AM. Every stay includes an evening bonfire, and a BBQ can be arranged on request for an added charge.

Leave early, keep the hill section unhurried, and the drive up is a genuinely pleasant start to the weekend rather than something to endure.
