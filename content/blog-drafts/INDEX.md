## How to review

- Mark up changes directly in the files, or tell me per file what to change.
- The header block (Title/Slug/Category/Meta) is NOT posted as body content — it maps to
  separate fields. Meta description goes into the Excerpt field. Meta title is the same as Title
  unless you want dedicated meta fields added to the schema.
- Every stay reference follows CLAUDE.md: bonfire included, BBQ paid add-on on request,
  check-in 12:00 PM, check-out 11:00 AM.

## Known limitations to decide on

1. No internal links. The renderer shows `[text](url)` as literal text, so the internal-linking
   strategy (link to Home / Stay / Book / related posts) cannot be done until the renderer is
   upgraded to parse links. Options: (a) I upgrade the renderer to support links + numbered
   lists, then add links, or (b) publish without inline links and rely on the fixed "Book a
   Stay" CTA that already sits at the bottom of every post.
2. Numbered steps in files 5 and 9 render as plain short paragraphs ("1. Drive to..."). Reads
   fine, but if you want real formatting that is part of the renderer upgrade above.
3. Body length is ~700 to 970 words. I can expand any to the full 1000 to 1200 if you want more
   depth on specific ones.
